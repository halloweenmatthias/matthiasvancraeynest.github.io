Juan Ramón Gómez García (he/him)

Hello! My name is Juan Ramón Gómez García, and I am a PhD student at the IMJ-PRG under the supervision of Adrien Brochier and Emmanuel Wagner, within the Groups, Representations and Geometry team.

My research focuses on Quantum Topology, with a particular emphasis on skein theory with defects. Currently, I am also investigating categorified quantum link invariants and their connections to q-holonomic systems. If you find any of this interesting, don't hesitate to drop me a line!

I am one of the organizers of the IMJ-PRG Preprint Seminar and a member of the BDD (feel free to contact me if you are considering doing your thesis at the IMJ-PRG!).
